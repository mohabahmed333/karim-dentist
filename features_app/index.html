Rules every message follows

Whatever triggers a message, it waits in one queue and passes the same checks before it is sent. A message is either sent, held for later, or dropped with a recorded reason — and the reason is visible under Patient messages.

Booking confirmations

Templates submitted — waiting for Meta

The patient gets a WhatsApp message as soon as an appointment is booked, from any source.

WhenThe moment an appointment is saved — from the website booking form, the admin panel, the admin AI chat, or the WhatsApp assistant.

  • Written in the patient's language: the language of their last WhatsApp message, otherwise the script of their name, otherwise Arabic.
  • One confirmation per booking, however many times the form is submitted.
  • If the booking fails because the slot was just taken, no confirmation is queued.
  • A booking made during quiet hours is confirmed at 09:00.
  • A patient who has already had three messages today waits an hour.

For staffNothing to do. Every message and what happened to it is listed under Patient messages.

Day-before reminders

Templates submitted — waiting for Meta

The patient is reminded the day before, in their own language.

When24 hours before the appointment — only if that moment is more than an hour away. A booking for this afternoon gets the confirmation alone.

  • The approved text says “tomorrow”. If a reminder is held past midnight — by quiet hours, say — it is dropped rather than sent naming the wrong day.
  • Moving the appointment withdraws the old reminder and sets a new one.
  • Cancelling, deleting, completing or marking a no-show withdraws it.
  • The patient can answer it with “cancel” or “الغاء” — see Cancel by replying.

Waitlist offers

No template yet

A cancelled slot is offered to the three longest-waiting patients; the first to accept books it.

WhenWhen a booked slot becomes free again — a cancellation, or a patient moved to another time.

  • Offered to the three patients who have waited longest whose preferred window includes the slot. No window means any time.
  • Just before sending, the slot is checked again. If someone already took it, the offer is not sent.
  • The first to reply gets it. The booking is atomic, so the others are told it was just taken — never double-booked.
  • The claim lasts 30 minutes. Slots less than an hour away are not offered.
  • Removing a patient from the waitlist withdraws any offer not yet sent.

For staffWaitlist — add a patient with an earliest and latest time they can come, or remove them.

Six-month check-up recalls

No template yet · marketing

A patient whose last visit was over six months ago, with nothing booked, is invited back.

WhenWhen a patient's most recent completed visit is more than 180 days ago and they have nothing booked.

  • Judged on the latest visit, across every way the patient's number has been written.
  • A patient who already has an appointment booked is never recalled.
  • One recall per lapse. A patient who comes back and lapses again is recalled again.
  • Marketing: sent only while “Recalls and review requests” is switched on.
  • Not looked for while notifications are Off, so nobody is silently marked as already recalled.

Review requests

No template yet · marketing

A patient who replies happily to their follow-up is asked for a review. Unhappy patients never are.

WhenWhen a patient replies to their follow-up and the assistant reads that reply as positive.

  • Pain mentioned alongside thanks counts as clinical, not positive.
  • Once per follow-up. Shares the marketing switch with recalls.

Voice notes answered

Needs the assistant on

A patient's voice message is transcribed and answered like text.

WhenA patient sends a voice message.

  • WhatsApp's provider transcribes it, and the assistant answers the transcript exactly as if it were typed.
  • If the transcript has not arrived yet, or is only music or silence — production already holds one reading “[outro jingle]” — it is not answered.
  • Clinical questions still go to staff.

Clinic knowledge answers

Needs the assistant on

The assistant answers prices, policies and aftercare from facts staff have written.

WhenA patient asks about prices, policies, directions or aftercare.

  • Each message searches Clinic knowledge in Arabic and English, and the closest facts are handed to the assistant.
  • It may state only those facts. If nothing matches, it passes the question to staff instead of guessing.
  • New entries start unpublished, and unpublished facts are invisible to the assistant.
  • Started with the clinic's 21 services and any FAQs already written.

For staffClinic knowledge — write the answer you would want a receptionist to give without checking, then publish it.

WhenStaff send one of the assistant's drafts, edited or not.

Patient control

What a patient can do to stop hearing from the clinic.

STOP opt-outs

Needs the database update

A patient who texts STOP or إيقاف receives no more clinic-initiated messages.

WhenA patient's whole message is an opt-out word.

  • Recorded before the assistant reads the message, and anything already queued for them is withdrawn.
  • Stops messages the clinic starts. If the patient writes in, they are still answered.
  • Works even while the assistant is off.
  • Only staff can let a patient hear from the clinic again.

Go-live order

Each step depends on the one before it.

  1. Merge the other session's branch

    Give it features_app/MERGE_INSTRUCTIONS.md. Two expected conflicts, both resolved toward main.

  2. Confirm the WhatsApp business account

    Its approved templates should be the clinic's.

  3. Get templates approved

    Wait for the four submitted; submit the other six. Each new one is added to templates.ts before it can send.

  4. Update the production database

    supabase db push --linked applies the nine changes. Everything starts switched off.

  5. Deploy

    Set CRON_SECRET in Vercel production, push main, and let it redeploy.

  6. Start the scheduler

    Create two Vault secrets, then run supabase/scripts/schedule_notifications_dispatch.sql once.

  7. Rehearse for a week

    Settings → Patient notifications → Dry run. Read the rendered messages under Patient messages.

  8. Send

    Switch to Send once every box that matters is ticked.

  9. Let the assistant act

    WhatsApp AI: Drafts → Replies → allow booking. Cancel-by-reply and waitlist claims need all three.

  10. Marketing last

    Recalls and review requests, only after their templates are approved and patients have agreed.

To stop everything at once: update patient_notification_settings set mode = 'off'; and update whatsapp_ai_settings set mode = 'off'; — effective within a minute, no deploy.

Known gaps

  • When the assistant cancels at a patient's request, the system cannot yet tell, so once a cancellation template exists that patient could hear it twice.
  • A patient offered a waitlist slot they did not take stays marked as offered and receives no further offers.
  • A patient who sent STOP cannot opt back in by message; only staff can.
  • Arabic search does not match word variants — “الأسنان” and “اسنان” may not find each other.
  • The new pages are reached by address or ⌘K, not the sidebar.
  • The end-to-end browser tests have not been run: local settings point at the live database, where they would create and delete real bookings.
